## Selected references

### PRIMARY — workspace: incident.io, incident detail (Timeline tab)

- [mobbin.com/screens/bf6f64ff-6ebc-4a7f-b354-f2a22066b03b](https://mobbin.com/screens/bf6f64ff-6ebc-4a7f-b354-f2a22066b03b)
- Saved: `references/01-incidentio-case-timeline.webp`

**Observed**
- One horizontal strip under the title holds the whole case state: a chevron stage rail
  (`Investigating › ⊙ Fixing › Monitoring › Documenting › Reviewing › Closed`) with past and future stages in grey
  and the current stage in black with a target icon, followed by two separate chips — severity (`▮▮ Major`) and
  elapsed time (`🕐 Ongoing for 24m 47s`).
- Breadcrumb ("Incidents") sits above a bold case title. No marketing furniture anywhere on the page.
- A single bordered summary card states the problem in plain language, with italic emphasis on the operative phrase.
- Tabs: Updates / **Timeline** / Actions / Follow-ups.
- A controls row above the timeline: date heading left; "Times shown in GMT+7", `Expand all`, `View all activity`, `Edit` right.
- Timeline rows: left-gutter time (`15:35`), a connected vertical spine with a colour-coded square icon tile per event,
  a bold one-line headline, and a collapse chevron.
- Nested update cards carry **state-change chips with the old value struck through**: `I̶n̶v̶e̶s̶t̶i̶g̶a̶t̶i̶n̶g̶ → ⊚ Fixing`,
  `C̶r̶i̶t̶i̶c̶a̶l̶ → ▮▮ Major`.
- Right rail of labelled key/value blocks divided by hairlines. **Timestamps** lists `Impact started at / Declared at /
  Identified at / Fixed at` with right-aligned datetimes.
- Honest empty values are shown rather than hidden: "Missing timestamps", "No attachments yet".

**Why this is the primary** — It is the same object as an LC Rescue case: a stateful incident with a prescribed
lifecycle, a chronological evidence record, a handful of decisive timestamps, and a human who must decide what to do
next. It solves my two worst problems directly. The struck-through state-change chip replaces a restated sentence with
the delta, and the Timestamps block is a date-comparison surface. It also demonstrates the discipline LC Rescue needs
most: showing an absent value honestly instead of omitting the row.

**Adaptation**
- Stage rail carries LC Rescue's real statuses: Investigate › Review plan › Coordinate › Recheck › Reviewed, with
  `Specialist review required` rendered as a terminal off-ramp rather than a sixth stage.
- Severity chip becomes the finding count; elapsed-time chip becomes **days to the presentation deadline**.
- Timestamps block becomes the Deadline & availability block (below).
- State-change chips carry `EUR → USD`, `NORTHSTAR TRADING LTD → TO ORDER OF MERIDIAN BANK`, and plan-revision deltas.

### SUPPORTING 1 — conflict display: Semrush, "Review your content"

- [mobbin.com/screens/ed82aafc-c4dc-442d-87fc-359f7347f940](https://mobbin.com/screens/ed82aafc-c4dc-442d-87fc-359f7347f940)
- Saved: `references/02-semrush-review-diff.webp`

**Observed**
- Two labelled columns, `Original` / `New`, each with a **line-number gutter**.
- Changed passages are tinted at block level — warm pink on the left, green on the right — with tighter inline
  highlights on the specific changed words inside the tinted block.
- Unchanged passages stay on plain background, so the eye goes straight to the difference.
- A decision control under each column: quiet `Keep original version` left, primary `Continue with new version` right.
- A summary metric sits above the diff (`Content score 90% ↑+8` against `Industry Avg. 60%`).

**Why** — LC Rescue's central promise is "every value is tied to the line it was read from". A line-number gutter
makes that literal in a way my current two-row table cannot. The block-tint-plus-inline-highlight pairing is exactly
right for "this document line is the problem, and *this token in it* is the conflict".

**Adaptation** — Columns become `Letter of credit requires` / `Commercial invoice says`. Gutter numbers are the real
line indices of the saved `.txt` source. The inline highlight marks only the conflicting token (`EUR`). No
accept/reject buttons: LC Rescue never edits a counterparty's document, it requests a reissue.

### SUPPORTING 2 — plan and audit history: Attio, record activity

- [mobbin.com/screens/297adfeb-8312-496b-8acf-41839a6cf775](https://mobbin.com/screens/297adfeb-8312-496b-8acf-41839a6cf775)
- Saved: `references/03-attio-grouped-changes.webp`

**Observed**
- Many individual field edits collapse into **one** timeline entry: "Alex Smith changed Stage and 7 other attributes".
- Expanding reveals one row per changed attribute with an arrow to the new value: `Stage → Meeting`,
  `Priority → Medium`, `Estimated contract value → $50,000.00`, `Projected close date → Sep 29th 2024`.
- A `Hide all changes` control collapses the group again.
- Entries are grouped under period headings ("This week", "June") with relative times ("3 minutes ago").

**Why** — The single worst defect in the current build is "Previous plans (14)", twelve of which restate the same
sentence verbatim under a raw ISO timestamp. Attio's grouping is the correction: one entry per *episode*, expandable
into per-field deltas.

**Adaptation** — "Remedy planner revised the plan 12 times" expands to rows showing only what changed between
revisions (timing verdict, next action, evidence set). Group under date headings; show relative time with the exact
timestamp on hover.

### LANDING — Harvey, homepage hero

- [mobbin.com/sites/sections/2e23b7a5-3438-48b3-ae2c-0b8e468e15a5](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2e23b7a5-3438-48b3-ae2c-0b8e468e15a5)
- Saved: `references/04-harvey-hero.webp`

**Observed**
- **Asymmetric hero.** A very large serif headline occupies the left two-thirds across three ragged lines; the
  supporting sentence and the single CTA sit to the *right*, vertically centred against the headline block — not
  stacked beneath it.
- The display face is a high-contrast transitional serif at **regular weight, not bold**, with tight leading, sharp
  bracketed serifs and ball terminals. UI chrome and body are set in a humanist sans. (Letterform description from the
  screenshot; the typeface is not named on it and I am not guessing one.)
- Off-white page, near-black text, one solid near-black button. No gradient, no card grid.
- Below the fold: the **real product UI**, full-bleed in a rounded frame, resting on a textured band — a dark sidebar,
  a page title, a stat (`49.5% ↑5%`), and two charts. It shows the actual working screen, not an abstraction.
- Critically, **the product screenshot uses the same serif for its page title** ("Whitford Lane's Command Center").
  The marketing identity is visibly the same identity as the application.

**Why this pairs with incident.io** — Both are restrained, near-monochrome, text-forward and free of decoration;
both treat dense professional content as the thing worth looking at. Harvey supplies the voice and the serif/sans
split; incident.io supplies the working anatomy. Neither fights the other, because neither relies on colour,
illustration or card chrome for its identity. Harvey also answers the brief's coherence requirement by example: the
serif crossing from the marketing page into the product title is precisely the mechanism that ties the two surfaces
together.

**Adaptation** — Same asymmetry and same serif-display/sans-UI split. The product shot is LC Rescue's own workspace
showing the real October 12 / October 16 conflict. No textured photographic band (no licensed imagery available, and
it would read as decoration here). Headline stays the supplied sentence.
