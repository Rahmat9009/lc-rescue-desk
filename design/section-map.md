# Section map — LC Rescue Desk

> **Superseded in part by [`corrections.md`](corrections.md) (2026-10-11).** That document corrects the
> typography recommendation (now Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3 + IBM Plex Mono), the brand colours
> (now `#023527` / `#478f81`, sampled from the supplied logo), the spacing scale, the 15px/16px body
> conflict, and the adaptive demonstration, which is now generated by the real domain functions rather
> than written. Read it alongside this file.

Every section below names its purpose, placement, reference, composition, the **actual** LC Rescue content it carries,
its interaction states, and the shared components it reuses. Content shown is real: fixture values from
`lib/domain.ts`, findings produced by `validate()`, and plan text produced by `basePlan()` / `lib/recovery.ts`.

Shared components are defined once in `design-spec.md` and referenced here by name.

# A. Landing (Persuade)

The landing is one argument in six moves: **a conflict exists → we find it → we draft the correction → a reply
arrives → the recommendation changes → a human decides, on the record.** Each section is one move. No section
introduces a visual identity the others do not share.

## A1. Landing navigation and brand

- **Purpose** — Identify the product; get to the workspace.
- **Placement** — Sticky top bar, 56px, hairline bottom border.
- **Reference** — Harvey (logo left, links centre, one solid CTA right).
- **Composition** — Mark + wordmark left. Right: `Open a case` (solid). No centre links — this product has four
  destinations and they live in the workspace rail, not here.
- **Content** — "LC Rescue Desk".
- **States** — Default; scrolled (border appears); `focus-visible` ring on both controls.
- **Shared** — `Button/primary`, `Brand`.
- **Note** — The current build's left rail is correct for the *workspace* but wrong for the landing, where it
  occupies 232px to show four destinations that are meaningless before a case exists. **Recommend: the landing has
  a top bar and no rail; the rail appears on entering the workspace.** This also fixes the mobile defect below.

## A2. Hero and product preview

- **Purpose** — State what the product decides, and show it deciding.
- **Placement** — Immediately under the bar. Asymmetric two-part block, then a full-bleed product frame.
- **Reference** — Harvey (asymmetry; serif display; real product shot below).
- **Composition** — Headline left across ~3 ragged lines at `display-1`. Supporting paragraph + CTA right,
  vertically centred against the headline. Below: the real workspace screenshot, rounded frame, bleeding to the
  grid edges.
- **Content** — Headline: *"When trade documents disagree, know what to do next."* Supporting: *"LC Rescue Desk
  compares letters of credit, invoices, and bills of lading, links conflicts to source evidence, and updates
  correction plans as replies arrive—with human approval throughout."* CTA `Open a case` → existing modal.
  Product shot: case `LC-2026-1042` at `Specialist review required`.
- **States** — Default; reduced-motion (no entrance); ≤860px the two parts stack and the product frame scrolls
  horizontally within a clipped container rather than shrinking to illegibility.
- **Shared** — `Button/primary`, `ProductFrame`, `SyntheticTag`.

## A3. Explanation of the document conflict

- **Purpose** — Make the reader understand the actual problem in ten seconds.
- **Placement** — First content section; off-white ground.
- **Reference** — Semrush (two labelled columns, line-number gutter, block tint + inline highlight).
- **Composition** — Section lede, then one `SourceDiff` showing a single real finding at full size. Three
  document chips above it (`Letter of credit · Commercial invoice · Bill of lading`) with the invoice chip active.
- **Content** — The real `invoice.currency` finding:
  - `LC-2026-1042.txt` line 3 → `currency: USD`
  - `INV-1042-v1.txt` line 4 → `currency: EUR` (`EUR` inline-highlighted)
  - Caption: "Invoice currency conflicts · Exporter reissues the document."
- **States** — Default; chip switch reveals the `bl.consignee` finding
  (`TO ORDER OF MERIDIAN BANK` vs `NORTHSTAR TRADING LTD`); reduced-motion crossfades instantly.
- **Shared** — `SourceDiff`, `DocChip`, `SyntheticTag`.

## A4. Investigation-to-correction workflow

- **Purpose** — Show the path from finding to approved request.
- **Placement** — Directly after A3, same ground, no new identity.
- **Reference** — Sequence step rail (horizontal numbered rail above **one** shared screenshot).
- **Composition** — A horizontal rail of four nodes joined by a hairline, each with a title and one line of copy;
  **one** product screenshot below, which swaps as a node is selected. Numbering is justified here: this is a real
  ordered sequence, and it is the same five-stage sequence the workspace header shows.
- **Content** — `1 Investigate` "Three documents read against the letter of credit." · `2 Review plan` "Exporter
  reissues the invoice; carrier reissues the bill of lading." · `3 Coordinate` "Approval binds the document versions
  it was given." · `4 Recheck` "Revised versions are verified independently; originals are retained."
- **States** — Node default / active / hover; keyboard arrow-key traversal; reduced-motion disables the crossfade.
- **Shared** — `StageRail` (the *same component* as the workspace header), `ProductFrame`.

## A5. Adaptive deadline-conflict demonstration

- **Purpose** — The distinctive claim: the recommendation changes when a reply arrives. This is the section the
  current landing is missing entirely, and it is the most important one on the page.
- **Placement** — Centrepiece. Full-width, inverted ground (deep forest) so it reads as the page's one emphasis.
- **Reference** — Mural (selectable list left, corresponding state right) + ClickUp (deadline rule) + incident.io
  (struck-through state-change chip).
- **Composition** — Left: two real correspondence quotations as selectable cards. Right: `DeadlineStrip` and the
  resulting recommendation. Selecting the second quotation visibly changes the strip and the recommendation; the
  previous recommendation is struck through in place.
- **Content** — all real, from the demo case (`asOf 2026-10-10`, deadline computed `2026-10-12`):
  - Exporter, 2026-10-11: *"We will send the corrected invoice on 2026-10-12."* → invoice available `2026-10-12`,
    timing `unknown`, recommendation stays "Approve the correction requests".
  - Carrier, 2026-10-11: *"We can reissue the corrected B/L on 2026-10-16."* → carrier available `2026-10-16`,
    timing **`conflict`**, recommendation becomes *"Escalate to a trade specialist and clarify the carrier timeline
    and presentation options with the relevant parties."*
  - Footer line: "Issuer authenticity and bank acceptance remain unconfirmed."
- **States** — Quote 1 selected (default) / quote 2 selected; reduced-motion swaps without the bar animation.
- **Shared** — `DeadlineStrip`, `QuoteCard`, `ChangeChip`, `RecommendationBlock`.

## A6. Evidence and implemented-scope explanation

- **Purpose** — Say precisely what is and is not implemented. For this product, candour *is* the pitch.
- **Placement** — After A5, back on off-white.
- **Reference** — Revolut verify checklist (one list, state-driven rows, honest status per row).
- **Composition** — Two columns of rows, each `label — state`. Not cards: a checklist reads as a claim set.
- **Content** — Implemented: source-grounded extraction with a required quote · currency and explicit-consignee
  conflicts · reference, amount and date checks · calculated presentation deadline · approval bound to document
  versions · version retention and independent recheck · deterministic correspondence assessment.
  Not implemented: PDF/OCR · document authenticity · full UCP/ISBP interpretation · bank holidays · recipient
  verification · external messaging · bank submission.
- **States** — Static.
- **Shared** — `ClaimRow`.
- **Note** — **Merge.** The current build splits this across a landing "capability band" and the Build-readiness
  tab's "What this first build covers", with overlapping text. One canonical scope list, surfaced in both places.

## A7. Closing action and footer

- **Purpose** — One last way in; the standing disclaimer.
- **Placement** — Page end.
- **Reference** — Harvey (restraint — one line, one button).
- **Composition** — Single centred line + CTA; hairline; one-row footer.
- **Content** — "Open the synthetic case and work the October 12 conflict end to end." → `Open a case`.
  Footer: "Prototype decision support · covered checks only · full presentation review required".
- **States** — Default.
- **Shared** — `Button/primary`.
- **Note** — **Remove** the current separate dark CTA band *and* capability band both competing for the page's
  emphasis. A5 is the emphasis; A7 is quiet.

# B. Workspace (Operate)

Scanability and stable positions outrank expression. The identity is carried by the same type, the same hairlines,
the same status colours and the same `SourceDiff` — not by restating the landing's drama.

## B1. Workspace navigation and saved-case selection

- **Purpose** — Move between the four destinations; switch case; get home.
- **Placement** — Left rail 232px, fixed. Top bar 56px with breadcrumb.
- **Reference** — incident.io (persistent rail; breadcrumb above a case title).
- **Composition** — Rail: brand (Home) · Rescue desk · Documents · Evidence · Build readiness · human-in-the-loop
  note · account. Top bar: `Home / Rescue desk`, private-workspace marker, refresh.
- **Content** — Real saved cases, e.g. `Demo · c18f1cf9 · Approval required`.
- **States** — Item default/hover/active; case select open; refreshing (the spinner must **not** move the page —
  this regression is fixed and must stay fixed); ≤860px rail collapses to icons; ≤560px **rail becomes a top
  drawer, not `display:none`**.
- **Shared** — `Rail`, `Breadcrumb`.
- **Note — defect found in this audit.** Below 560px the rail is `display:none`, so the brand Home action *and all
  four destinations* are unreachable; only the breadcrumb Home survives. Must be fixed.

## B2. Case header, status, and document versions

- **Purpose** — Say what this case is and where it stands, in one strip.
- **Placement** — Directly under the top bar. Fixed height; never reflows between statuses.
- **Reference** — incident.io status strip (stage chevrons + separate state chips).
- **Composition** — Row 1: case reference as title + mode chip + document-version chips. Row 2: `StageRail` +
  `findings` chip + `days to deadline` chip.
- **Content** — `LC-2026-1042` · `Demo · no model calls` · `LC v1 · INV v1 · B/L v1` ·
  Investigate › **Review plan** › Coordinate › Recheck › Reviewed · `2 findings` · `2 days to deadline`.
- **States** — Five stages; `Specialist review required` renders the rail greyed with a terminal off-ramp marker
  rather than inventing a sixth stage; `Covered checks passed` fills the rail.
- **Shared** — `StageRail`, `Chip`, `VersionChip`.
- **Note** — **Merge.** Document versions currently live only in the Documents tab; surfacing them here is what makes
  "approval binds these versions" legible at the moment of approving.

## B3. Latest validated action and its supporting evidence

- **Purpose** — The one thing to do next, and why it is believed.
- **Placement** — First block below the header. Left column, ~62% width.
- **Reference** — incident.io summary card; Melio (reason sits directly beneath the value it explains).
- **Composition** — Eyebrow `Latest validated action`; the action at `heading-1`; the reason; then **Supporting
  evidence** as quotation rows, each citing its origin; then **Unresolved** as plain rows; then the primary button.
- **Content** — "Escalate to a trade specialist and clarify the carrier timeline and presentation options with the
  relevant parties." Reason: "The cited carrier B/L correction availability 2026-10-16 falls after the saved
  presentation deadline 2026-10-12." Evidence: `Saved presentation deadline: 2026-10-12` ·
  *"We can reissue the corrected B/L on 2026-10-16."* Unresolved: three real items from `recoveryPlan.unresolved`.
- **States** — Per status: new / approval / waiting / received / reviewed / stopped. Where no action is available,
  the button is absent — not disabled.
- **Shared** — `RecommendationBlock`, `QuoteRow`, `Button/primary`.
- **Note** — **Removes a three-way duplication.** This text currently appears in the latest-action band, the
  strategy card and the correspondence card. It appears **once** here; the correspondence card keeps only the
  per-reply assessment that produced it.

## B4. Deadline and counterparty availability comparison

- **Purpose** — Make the timing conflict visible instead of narrated. The product's distinctive moment.
- **Placement** — Right of B3, ~38%. Same top edge.
- **Reference** — incident.io Timestamps block (labelled rows, right-aligned dates, honest empty values) +
  ClickUp (deadline rule).
- **Composition** — A short horizontal date axis spanning the relevant window with a solid rule at the deadline.
  Each availability is a point on the axis; any point past the rule is drawn in `conflict`. Beneath: the same values
  as labelled rows with right-aligned dates, so the information survives without the graphic.
- **Content** — Axis Oct 10 → Oct 17. Deadline rule `2026-10-12`. Points: `Invoice available 2026-10-12` (at the
  rule), `B/L available 2026-10-16` (past it, conflict). Rows: `Case date 2026-10-10` · `Presentation deadline
  2026-10-12` · `Invoice available 2026-10-12` · `B/L available 2026-10-16` · `Timing conflict`.
- **States** — `feasible` / `conflict` / `unknown`. When a date is unknown the row reads `Not established` and no
  point is plotted — never a guessed position.
- **Shared** — `DeadlineStrip`, `KeyValueRow`.
- **Note** — **New, and the single highest-value addition.** The scenario that makes this product distinctive is
  currently only prose.

## B5. Findings and actual-versus-required values

- **Purpose** — Every conflict, tied to its source line.
- **Placement** — Full width below B3/B4.
- **Reference** — Semrush diff; Melio (red dot on the row, reason under the value).
- **Composition** — One `SourceDiff` per finding: required side labelled with the LC and its line number, actual side
  labelled with the document and its line number, the conflicting token inline-highlighted, the consequence beneath.
- **Content** — `Invoice currency conflicts` — LC line 3 `currency: USD` / INV line 4 `currency: EUR`; owner Exporter.
  `Consignee conflicts with explicit LC instruction` — LC line 5 / BL line 4; owner Carrier.
- **States** — Blocker / review severity; zero findings → `Covered checks passed` with the scope caveat; `new` →
  empty state inviting investigation.
- **Shared** — `SourceDiff`, `OwnerChip`.

## B6. Source document viewer and extracted quotations

- **Purpose** — Read the document; see each extracted value against the line it came from.
- **Placement** — Documents destination.
- **Reference** — Mistral Document Intelligence (source left with pager; right pane with output-mode tabs;
  **line-numbered** view) + Airwallex (source left / fields right).
- **Composition** — Document tabs across the top. Left: source text with a **line-number gutter**, the lines that
  produced an extraction subtly marked. Right: extracted fields, each `name / value / quote`, clicking one scrolls
  and highlights its source line.
- **Content** — `LC-2026-1042.txt` seven fields; `INV-1042-v1.txt` four; `BL-1042-v1.txt` four. Real quotes.
- **States** — Per document; `new` (no extraction yet); revised versions available.
- **Shared** — `SourceView`, `FieldRow`.
- **Note** — The line-number gutter is what finally makes the landing's "linked to the line it came from" claim
  literally true in the product.

## B7. Correction drafts and human approval

- **Purpose** — Review what will be requested, from whom, and approve it.
- **Placement** — Below findings, left column.
- **Reference** — Revolut verify checklist (one row per party, state-driven) + Airwallex (approve anchored to the panel).
- **Composition** — One row per issuer: document chip, party, instruction, state. Drafts in a disclosure. Approval
  button anchored at the panel foot with the binding statement beside it.
- **Content** — `INV · Exporter · Review and reissue the invoice · Awaiting approval`;
  `B/L · Carrier · Review and reissue the bill of lading · Awaiting approval`. Binding statement: "Approval binds
  invoice v1 and bill of lading v1. Nothing is sent to a counterparty."
- **States** — Awaiting approval / approved (timestamped) / superseded by newer correspondence (the real
  `approvalRequired` case, which must be visibly distinct).
- **Shared** — `ClaimRow`, `OwnerChip`, `Disclosure`, `Button/primary`.
- **Note** — Parties are **parallel**, so the current `01` / `02` markers misrepresent them as a sequence. Use the
  document code (`INV`, `B/L`).

## B8. Revised document uploads and recheck

- **Purpose** — Take new versions in and verify them.
- **Placement** — Replaces B7 at status `waiting` / `received`.
- **Reference** — Elicit upload modal (per-file progress and checkmarks).
- **Composition** — One drop target per expected document with per-file state; recheck button enabled only when both
  are present. Demo mode shows the three simulated reply scenarios instead.
- **Content** — "Upload revised Commercial invoice" / "Upload revised Bill of lading"; or
  `Receive synthetic corrected versions` / `Carrier delay` / `Buyer waiver only`.
- **States** — Empty / one of two / both / invalid (non-`.txt` or >16KB, with the real rule stated) / rechecking.
- **Shared** — `DropTarget`, `Button/primary`.

## B9. Correspondence entry and assessment

- **Purpose** — Paste a reply; see what was extracted from it and what that changes.
- **Placement** — Below B7/B8, left column, measure-capped.
- **Reference** — incident.io update card (author, time, body, resulting state chips).
- **Composition** — Sender select + textarea + submit. Each saved reply renders as a card: sender and date, the
  verbatim text, then the typed statements extracted from it, then the chips for what changed.
- **Content** — `carrier · 2026-10-11` — *"We can reissue the corrected B/L on 2026-10-16."* →
  `commitment · bl · 2026-10-16`; chips: `timing: unknown → conflict`, `plan revised`, `approval invalidated`.
  Standing line: "User-provided correspondence · authenticity unverified. No messages are sent."
- **States** — Empty / drafting / submitting / assessed / reassessed without model calls.
- **Shared** — `QuoteCard`, `ChangeChip`, `Button/primary`.

## B10. Previous plans and correspondence history

- **Purpose** — Show how the recommendation evolved, without 14 restatements.
- **Placement** — Collapsed disclosure below B9.
- **Reference** — Attio grouped changes (primary) + Reddit revision compare.
- **Composition** — One entry per *episode*, not per write: "Remedy planner revised the plan 12 times · 2026-10-11".
  Expanding shows only the deltas, as `ChangeChip` rows. A plan that genuinely differs gets its own entry.
- **Content** — Episode 1: "Plan created · timing unknown". Episode 2: "Clarify missing correction dates".
  Episode 3: "timing `unknown → conflict`; next action changed; evidence +1 quotation". Approval state per episode.
- **States** — Collapsed (default) / expanded / single-revision case (no grouping needed).
- **Shared** — `Disclosure`, `ChangeChip`, `RelativeTime`.
- **Note** — **The worst current defect.** Fourteen entries, twelve identical, each headed by a raw ISO timestamp
  such as `2026-10-11T04:57:52.925Z`. Raw ISO strings must never be a heading; use `RelativeTime` with the exact
  value on hover.

## B11. Audit trail and case export

- **Purpose** — The complete record, and a file of it.
- **Placement** — Collapsed disclosure, last block.
- **Reference** — incident.io timeline (gutter time, icon spine, one-line headline, collapsible detail).
- **Composition** — Date headings; gutter times; one-line headlines; detail on expand. Export sits in the case
  header, not buried in this disclosure.
- **Content** — Real events: `Case opened` · `Correction plan ready` · `Recovery plan updated` · `Saved
  correspondence reassessed`, each with its actor (`Remedy planner`, `Verifier`, `Human reviewer`).
- **States** — Collapsed / expanded / filtered to decisions only (44 events is too many unfiltered).
- **Shared** — `Timeline`, `ActorChip`, `Disclosure`.
- **Note** — **Merge B10 into B11** as two tabs of one History block — `Decisions` and `All activity` — mirroring
  incident.io's `Timeline` / `View all activity`. They are the same record at two resolutions.

## B12. Sponsor evidence and readiness

- **Purpose** — Attach external proof; show what still needs configuring.
- **Placement** — Its own destination.
- **Reference** — Revolut verify checklist.
- **Composition** — One checklist. Each row: requirement, current state, input or link.
- **Content** — neatlogs trace URL · Entire checkpoint URL + ID · cfo.ai plan URL · Gemini model configured.
  Real states from `readiness()`.
- **States** — Requires action / configured / verified; save success and failure.
- **Shared** — `ClaimRow`, `Input`, `Button/primary`.
- **Note** — **Merge** today's two destinations (Evidence, Build readiness) into one **Readiness**. They are one
  checklist split across two pages, and the scope text duplicates A6.

## B13. Case-opening modal

- **Purpose** — Start a case from fixtures or real text.
- **Placement** — Centred dialog over the current page, which must not change beneath it.
- **Reference** — Elicit upload modal; Airwallex (primary action bottom-right).
- **Composition** — Title; two mode options; the mode's body; footer with `Cancel` and the primary action.
- **Content** — Demo: "Two deliberate conflicts — a $48,000 invoice and a presentation deadline two days out."
  Live: case date, three text inputs, the `.txt` ≤16KB rule, and the real readiness warning when unconfigured.
- **States** — Demo / live; live-unconfigured (primary disabled, reason stated); submitting; failed (modal stays
  open, error inside it); cancelled (returns to the page underneath, unchanged).
- **Shared** — `Dialog`, `Button/primary`, `DropTarget`.

## B14. Empty, loading, error, and mobile states

- **Purpose** — Make the unhappy paths part of the system, not afterthoughts.
- **Reference** — incident.io ("Missing timestamps", "No attachments yet" shown rather than hidden).
- **Composition and content**
  - **Empty — no cases:** the landing is the empty state. No separate screen.
  - **Empty — case not investigated:** "Nothing compared yet" + the investigate action.
  - **Empty — no findings:** "Covered checks passed" + the scope caveat, never a celebration.
  - **Empty — value unknown:** the row stays and reads `Not established`.
  - **Loading:** skeletons that match the final block's geometry. The global "Loading desk" bar must never shift
    layout or change the visible page.
  - **Error:** inline, in the block that failed, stating what was kept — "Live action failed. Your previous saved
    case is intact." Never a toast for a state-changing failure.
  - **Mobile:** single column; `SourceDiff` stacks required-over-actual, keeping both gutters;
    `DeadlineStrip` becomes the labelled rows only; rail becomes a drawer.
- **States** — As listed.
- **Shared** — `Skeleton`, `InlineError`, `EmptyState`.

## Summary of structural recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add B4 Deadline & availability** | The distinctive scenario is currently prose only |
| 2 | **Merge B10 into B11** as `Decisions` / `All activity` | One record at two resolutions |
| 3 | **Merge Evidence + Build readiness → Readiness** | One checklist split across two pages |
| 4 | **Merge A6 with Build-readiness scope text** | Same claims, two wordings |
| 5 | **Remove the landing capability band** | A3–A5 demonstrate; the band only asserts |
| 6 | **Remove the duplicate recommendation in the strategy card** | Stated three times |
| 7 | **Landing uses a top bar, not the workspace rail** | Four destinations are meaningless pre-case; also fixes mobile |
| 8 | **Surface document versions in B2** | Makes "approval binds these versions" legible where it matters |
| 9 | **Fix: rail `display:none` ≤560px** | Home and all destinations unreachable on mobile |
| 10 | **Fix: never use a raw ISO timestamp as a heading** | `2026-10-11T04:57:52.925Z` is not a label |

Sections deliberately **not** invented to fill the brief's list: there is no testimonial, metrics, pricing,
integrations or team section, because the product has no such content and manufacturing it would be a false claim.
